Three decisions carry it. Every field is readonly, so an event cannot be edited after it is emitted — a turn's history stays a record rather than a mutable buffer. kind is a closed union of five strings, not an open one, so a vendor cannot smuggle its own message taxonomy across the boundary. And generation travels on every event, which is what lets a superseded answer be thrown away when the user interrupts.

SessionId and GenerationId are aliases, not classes. They cost nothing at runtime and exist so that the fields read as what they are wherever the shape is copied.

3. The five kinds, and what each one is for

Five is a deliberate number: enough for a voice policy to decide what to say out loud, what to swallow, and when the turn is over.

KindWhat it carriesWhat a voice policy does with it
assistant_textA piece of the answer.Speak it.
reasoningThe agent thinking out loud.Swallow it. Never spoken.
toolA tool call or its result.Swallow it; may be shown on screen.
terminalThe turn finished normally.Close the turn, stop listening for this generation.
errorThe turn failed.Close the turn and report.

Only the first one is ever heard. The other four exist so the screen and the policy can tell “nothing to say yet” apart from “nothing more coming” — a distinction that is invisible if a stream carries only text.

6. What has to happen next

  1. Pick one session-id type. string in TypeScript, UUID in pydantic_models/models.py. One of the two has to move; a wire that only happens to line up is not an agreement.
  2. Decide whether the event carries a session at all. submit( text, generation ) is never given one, so the adapter that actually runs cannot fill the field. Either submit() starts handing it down, or the field leaves the event.
  3. Bring the adapter copy up to five kinds. It emits assistant_text and terminal only. The other three are declared everywhere else and produced nowhere.
  4. Give the agreement a test. Nothing fails today when the three copies drift — that is why they drifted. This is Stage 2's reconciliation item.
  5. Make one consumer read them. Until SpokenOutputPolicy actually filters a stream, the five kinds are a vocabulary nobody has spoken.
